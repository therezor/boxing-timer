# Boxing Timer

A round timer for boxing, MMA and interval training that installs on iPhone as a home-screen app, works fully offline, and plays its bells **on top of your music** instead of stopping it.

**Open:** https://therezor.github.io/boxing-timer/

## Install on iPhone

1. Open the link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Launch it from the home screen once while online. After that it works in airplane mode.

## Using it with music

- Start Spotify / Apple Music first, then open the timer. Bells mix over the music.
- Bells follow the **Ring/Silent switch**. If you hear nothing, turn Silent Mode off.
- **Settings → Sounds → With music** (iOS 17+): **Mix** (default) plays bells over the music. **Duck** uses iOS's short-sound mode, which may lower the music while a bell plays; try it if bells are hard to hear.
- Keep the timer on screen while you train. It keeps the screen awake, but iOS pauses web apps when the screen is locked or you switch apps. When you come back, the clock is still correct.

## Settings

Everything is under the sliders button (top right) and is saved on the device:

- **Presets:** Pro boxing (12 × 3:00), Amateur (3 × 3:00), MMA (5 × 5:00), Tabata (8 × 0:20 / 0:10), plus your own. Type a name and tap **Save**.
- **Timing:** rounds, round length, rest, get-ready countdown, warning before the round ends, countdown beeps at the end of rest. Hold **+**/**−** to change values quickly.
- **Sounds:** choose the sound for round start, round end, last round end, warning, halfway and countdown, with ▶ to preview each one. There is also a volume control.
- **Display:** colour for each phase, keep screen awake, show total time left.

## Development

Plain HTML/CSS/JS with no build step. To run it locally:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

- The sounds are synthesized with the Web Audio API (`audio.js`). There are no audio files, and no `<audio>` elements, which would take over the iOS audio session and pause music.
- `sw.js` caches every file. **Bump `CACHE` in `sw.js` whenever you change a file**, so that installed copies update.
- Icons are generated by `node tools/make-icons.mjs`.
